Explain It

1. In your own words, how does your app change a sound wave into binary? Name the three steps.

My app converts a sound wave into binary through three steps: sampling, quantization, and binary encoding. It measures the wave at different points, rounds the values to levels, and converts them into binary numbers.

2. Does 2-bit or 6-bit encoding show more detail in Test A?

6 bits gives more detail because it has 64 levels, while 2 bits only has 4 levels. More levels allow the app to represent the wave's amplitude more accurately.

3.Which test has the longest wavelength? How is wavelength connected to frequency?

Test B has the longest wavelength because it has the lowest frequency of 200 Hz. As frequency increases, wavelength decreases.

4. What was the hardest stage to build, and how did you fix it?

The hardest part was converting the wave's amplitude into binary because the calculations needed to be accurate. I solved it by checking the formulas, testing different inputs, and comparing the results with the given example.
